# Project Description

A 3D Physics obstacle course game built in Godot with strong influences from mini-golf with the following modifications.

## World and Level

- The game world takes strong inspiration from the "Toy Story" Movies mixed with the video games "Re-Volt(1999)" & "AirFix Dogfight"(2000). 
  - Scale: 1:32
  - Description: The player will feel small compared to every day objects
- The obstacle course is built from a variety of everyday household objects. 
  - Examples Objects.: utensils, books, rulers, stationary, children's blocks and toys, boxes, cups, stools, board game boxes, various IR remotes, random stuff found in the household junk drawer.
  - Make the level traversable by connecting the random every day objects into a continues yet meandering path utilizing all available spatial axes.
  - There is a start and goal point in the obstacle course.
  - There are 3 mandatory equidistant checkpoints along the path of the course.
- The standard level environment is set on the floor of a large house Filled with various furniture.
  - Example Furniture: dining table, bed, chairs, crt tv, walls, posters, tube-lights, ceiling fans.

## Player Controller
- The camera is focused at an angle, 45 degrees, looking at the obstacle course from a height making the obstacle course feel small.
- The Player uses a variety of household stationary objects (such as pens, pencils, brushes) like a golf club to hit a pink soft rectangular eraser down the course.
  - The player may also try to slide the eraser instead of whacking it.
  - If the eraser falls off the course and touches the floor, reset to last checkpoint.
  - The soft eraser has high friction.
